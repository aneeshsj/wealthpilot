# Sector Underwriting Note: Retail Trade

**ASHVA CAPITAL INTERNAL CREDIT POLICY MANUAL**
**SECTION 4: INDUSTRY-SPECIFIC UNDERWRITING STANDARDS**
**SUBJECT: RETAIL TRADE SME LENDING**

---

### 4.1 Purpose and Scope
This policy governs the credit evaluation, risk grading, and exposure management for commercial borrowers classified under the Retail Trade SME sector. All underwriters, credit committee members, and automated decision engines of Ashva Capital are bound by the limits, ratios, and covenants set forth in this document. Any deviation requires formal sign-off from Chief Credit Officer Arthur Pendelton or Senior Risk Director Beatrice Vance.

### 4.2 Working Capital Cycle
1. **Standard Cycle Length:** The typical cash-to-cash working capital cycle for approved Retail Trade SME borrowers is established at **40 days**. 
2. **Measurement:** Underwriters shall calculate the operating cycle as Days Inventory Outstanding (DIO) plus Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) minus Days Payables Outstanding (DPO), based on the trailing twelve months (TTM) audited or reviewed financial statements.
3. **Threshold Exception:** Any applicant exhibiting a working capital cycle exceeding **65 days** shall be subjected to a mandatory liquidity audit by the internal risk analytics team before credit file submission.

### 4.3 Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)
1. **Base Ratio:** The standard sector DSCR floor for Retail Trade SME lending is **1.25x**, calculated on net operating income after owner compensation and current debt service obligations.
2. **Application:** No underwriting exception shall be granted for any retail applicant operating below a **1.25x** historic or projected DSCR over the proposed loan tenor.

### 4.4 Seasonality Adjustments and Peak Cycles
1. **Seasonal Window:** The Retail Trade SME sector experiences its primary seasonal cash-flow concentration and inventory build-up from **October to December** annually.
2. **Advance Rate Adjustments:** During the pre-peak accumulation window (August through September), underwriters may approve a temporary revolving facility advance rate increase of up to **15%** against eligible inventory, provided the borrower maintains a minimum current ratio of **1.35x**.
3. **Repayment Structuring:** For borrowers generating more than **50%** of annual gross revenue during the October to December peak, principal repayment schedules must incorporate seasonal principal reduction holidays or balloon amortizations aligned with post-peak cash conversion ending by **February 28**.

### 4.5 Primary Causes of Sector Distress
Underwriters must specifically evaluate and document mitigants against the two most common operational causes of default in Retail Trade SME lending:
1. **Margin Compression via Unhedged Supplier Cost Increases:** Inability to pass wholesale cost inflation directly to end consumers due to elastic demand or big-box retail price matching. Underwriters must verify a minimum three-year historical gross margin stability of **22%** or higher.
2. **Obsolescence Risk in Aging Inventory:** Accumulation of slow-moving stock that cannot be liquidated without margin-destroying markdowns. Underwriters must discount inventory values by **30%** when calculating tangible net worth and borrowing base availability.

### 4.6 Acceptable Collateral Types
Credit facilities in this sector shall be secured by a first-priority general security agreement (GSA) over all corporate assets, supplemented by the following prioritized collateral classes:
1. **First-Ranking Charge over Commercial Real Estate:** Valued by an Ashva Capital-approved independent appraiser within the preceding **12 months**, accepted at a maximum loan-to-value (LTV) of **65%**.
2. **First-Ranking Charge over Inventories and Accounts Receivable:** Subject to a monthly borrowing base certificate. Eligible inventory shall exclude goods held on consignment or aged over **180 days**.
3. **Unconditional Personal Guarantees:** Mandatory from all beneficial owners holding an equity stake of **20%** or greater, backed by a statement of net worth verified via personal tax returns.

### 4.7 Portfolio Exposure Limits and Tenor Caps
1. **Portfolio Cap:** Total aggregate credit exposure to the Retail Trade SME sector shall not exceed **12 percent** of the total Ashva Capital loan book. As of the current reporting cycle, sector exposure stands at **8.9 percent**, leaving an active deployment capacity of **3.1 percent**.
2. **Maximum Tenor:** The maximum allowable repayment tenor for term loans extended under this sector policy is **36 months**. Working capital revolving facilities shall be subject to annual review and mandatory clean-down of at least **15 consecutive days** within any rolling 12-month period.
