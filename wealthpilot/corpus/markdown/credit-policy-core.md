# Ashva Capital Credit Policy Manual: Core Underwriting Standards

ASHVA CAPITAL INTERNAL CREDIT POLICY MANUAL  
DOCUMENT REF: CPM-SME-2026-V4  
APPLICABILITY: ALL SME TERM LOAN UNDERWRITING  

---

### SECTION 1: PURPOSE AND DEFINITIONS

1.1 This manual establishes the binding core underwriting standards for all Small and Medium Enterprise (SME) term loans administered by Ashva Capital ("the Lender"). Underwriters and automated systems are strictly bound by these provisions.

1.2 "Operating History" means the continuous duration of commercial operations of the applicant business, measured in completed calendar months from the date of formal registration or commencement of trading to the date of loan application.

1.3 "Debt-Service Coverage Ratio" (DSCR) is defined as EBITDA divided by Total Annual Debt Service. 
(a) EBITDA represents earnings before interest, tax, depreciation, and amortization, derived from verified financial statements or tax filings.
(b) Total Annual Debt Service is defined as the existing annual debt service plus the first twelve monthly instalments on the proposed loan.
(c) The first twelve monthly instalments on the proposed loan shall be calculated on a reducing balance at the assessment rate of 14.00 percent per annum.

1.4 "Leverage" is defined as the ratio of total debt (existing debt plus the proposed loan) to EBITDA.

1.5 "Current Ratio" is defined as current assets divided by current liabilities, derived from the most recent balance sheet.

1.6 "Lending Base Rate" is established at 9.50 percent per annum. All final pricing shall be set at or above this base rate in accordance with risk-grade matrices.

---

### SECTION 2: ELIGIBILITY AND THRESHOLD STANDARDS

2.1 **Operating History:** The minimum operating history required for any SME term loan is 24 months. Businesses with an operating history of less than 24 months are ineligible for credit.

2.2 **Debt-Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR):** The minimum acceptable DSCR is 1.25x, or the applicable sector floor where that sector floor is higher than 1.25x.

2.3 **Leverage Limits:** Maximum leverage shall not exceed 6.0 times EBITDA. Existing debt plus the proposed loan combined must satisfy this threshold.

2.4 **Current-Ratio Floor:** The minimum current-ratio floor is 1.10. Applications exhibiting a current ratio below 1.10 fail this standard.

---

### SECTION 3: CREDIT GRADING AND BUREAU STANDARDS

3.1 **Bureau Score Thresholds:** Applicants shall be assigned a credit grade based on their minimum bureau score as follows:
(a) Grade A1: Minimum score of 750
(b) Grade A2: Minimum score of 700
(c) Grade B1: Minimum score of 650
(d) Grade B2: Minimum score of 600
(e) Grade C1: Minimum score of 550
(f) Grade C2: Minimum score of 500
(g) Grade D1: Minimum score of 450
(h) Grade D2: Any bureau score below 450.

3.2 **Adverse Bureau Record:** A bureau score below 450 constitutes an adverse bureau record.

3.3 **Past Delinquency:** A past delinquency is defined strictly as either:
(a) Any 90-day delinquency recorded in the credit history; or
(b) A written-off amount of 500,000 rupees or more.

---

### SECTION 4: DECISION ROUTING MATRIX

4.1 **Declined Applications:** An application shall be declined automatically by the underwriting system or manually by an underwriter when any of the following conditions are met:
(a) DSCR is below 1.00;
(b) The bureau record is adverse (score below 450);
(c) The business has operated for less than 24 months; or
(d) The assigned grade is D2.

4.2 **Borderline Applications:** An application is classified as borderline and must be referred to a human reviewer when any of the following conditions apply:
(a) Any standard outside of the declination triggers is missed;
(b) There is a past delinquency present in the record;
(c) The requested tenor is longer than allowed by the grade or sector;
(d) The collateral is worth less than the cover required by the grade;
(e) Any figure needed to test a standard is missing; or
(f) The assigned grade is C2 or D1.

4.3 **Approvable Applications:** An application that meets all core thresholds, exhibits no past delinquencies, features no missing data, and does not fall under clauses 4.1 or 4.2 is classified as approvable.

---

### SECTION 5: AUTOMATED VS. HUMAN DECISIONING

5.1 **Automatic Approval Criteria:** The underwriting system may approve an application automatically if and only if:
(a) The application is classified as approvable under clause 4.3;
(b) The loan amount is no more than 1,000,000 rupees; and
(c) The assigned grade is C1 or better (A1, A2, B1, B2, C1).

5.2 **Human Sign-Off Requirement:** Everything outside the parameters of clause 5.1—including all borderline applications, all declines requiring manual review, and all loans exceeding the automated threshold—requires formal human sign-off.

---

### SECTION 6: APPROVAL AUTHORITY BY LOAN SIZE

6.1 Approval authority for SME term loans is strictly partitioned by loan size as follows:
(a) **Credit Officer:** Loans up to 1,000,000 rupees.
(b) **Credit Committee:** Loans above 1,000,000 rupees and up to 5,000,000 rupees.
(c) **Board Credit Committee:** Loans of any size larger than 5,000,000 rupees.

6.2 **Named Signatory Mandate:** No loan above 5,000,000 rupees may be approved without a named human signatory from the Board Credit Committee. Blanket or system-only approvals are strictly prohibited for this tier.

### SECTION 7: WORKED DSCR CALCULATION EXAMPLE

7.1 Underwriters shall execute debt-service capacity assessments strictly in accordance with the following worked formula structure:

* **Given Financial Parameters:**
  * EBITDA: 2,400,000 rupees
  * Existing Annual Debt Service: 600,000 rupees
  * Proposed Loan: 2,000,000 rupees over a 48-month tenor
  * First twelve monthly instalments at the assessment rate of 14.00 percent per annum: 655,835 rupees

* **Calculation Formula:**
  $$\text{DSCR} = \frac{\text{EBITDA}}{\text{Existing Annual Debt Service} + \text{First Year Proposed Instalments}}$$

* **Numerical Substitution:**
  $$\text{DSCR} = \frac{2,400,000}{600,000 + 655,835}$$

* **Result:**
  $$\text{DSCR} = \frac{2,400,000}{1,255,835} = 1.91x$$

7.2 The resulting DSCR of 1.91x exceeds the minimum policy threshold of 1.25x, satisfying Section 2.2 of this manual.
