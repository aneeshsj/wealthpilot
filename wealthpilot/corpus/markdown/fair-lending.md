# Fair Lending and Non-Discrimination Standard

**ASHVA CAPITAL CREDIT POLICY MANUAL**
**SECTION 4: FAIR LENDING AND NON-DISCRIMINATION STANDARD**
**DOCUMENT ID: CP-POL-2024-04**
**EFFECTIVE DATE: OCTOBER 1, 2024**
**OWNER: CHIEF RISK OFFICER & CHIEF CREDIT OFFICER**

---

### 4.1 PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This standard binds all Ashva Capital underwriters, automated underwriting engines, credit committee members, and risk analytics personnel. Compliance with this section is mandatory across all retail, SME, and commercial lending portfolios. Failure to adhere to these fair lending provisions constitutes a direct violation of internal employment and fiduciary duties, resulting in immediate disciplinary review by the Ashva Capital Disciplinary Committee, up to and including termination and reporting to relevant statutory authorities.

### 4.2 PROHIBITED PROTECTED ATTRIBUTES
Underwriters and automated decision systems shall not collect, review, weight, or otherwise consider the following protected attributes of any primary applicant, co-applicant, guarantor, or director. These attributes must be scrubbed from all credit decisioning data pipelines prior to feature extraction:

1. **Religion:** Any explicit or inferred religious affiliation, denomination, or observance.
2. **Caste:** Any lineage, tribe, sub-caste, or traditional social stratification category.
3. **Gender:** Male, female, non-binary, or any other gender identity or expression.
4. **Marital Status:** Single, married, divorced, widowed, or cohabitating status.
5. **Age:** Chronological age, provided the applicant has reached the statutory age of majority (fixed at 18 years across all operational jurisdictions).
6. **Disability:** Any physical, sensory, cognitive, or psychological impairment.
7. **Region of Origin:** Birthplace, native state, or ancestral geographic domicile within or outside the operating country.

### 4.3 PROHIBITED PROXY VARIABLES
To prevent indirect discrimination, variables that exhibit a high statistical correlation with the protected attributes enumerated in Section 4.2 are classified as prohibited proxies. The following data points must never be ingested by the credit scoring model or viewed by manual underwriters:

1. **Postal Code Alone:** Raw postal codes, ZIP codes, or sector codes. *Exception:* Postal codes may be utilized strictly when aggregated into a standardized "Geographic Risk Tier" (Tier 1 through Tier 5) generated by the Ashva Capital Risk Analytics Unit, provided the tiering model undergoes disparate-impact testing under Section 4.4 and strips out any correlation with caste, region of origin, or religion exceeding an $R^2$ threshold of 0.15.
2. **Applicant First Name:** Raw string values of an applicant's given name. *Exception:* Name strings may be passed through an entity-resolution parser solely for the purpose of matching against the internal Sanctions and Politically Exposed Persons (PEP) watchlist managed by the Compliance Department. Underwriters and credit scoring models are restricted from accessing name-derived demographic inferences.
3. **Institution of Education:** The specific name, brand, or tier of high schools, colleges, or universities attended by the applicant. *Exception:* Educational attainment may be captured strictly as a binary ordinal variable: (a) Degree attained, or (b) No degree attained.
4. **Language of Application:** The specific human language, script, or dialect selected by the applicant to complete the loan application form.

### 4.4 DISPARATE-IMPACT TESTING AND CADENCE
The Model Risk Management (MRM) team, under the direct oversight of Chief Risk Officer Marcus Vance, shall execute disparate-impact and adverse-effect testing on all active credit decisioning models and underwriting rulesets.

1. **Cadence:** Disparate-impact testing must be conducted on a strict quarterly schedule, no later than the 15th calendar day following the close of each financial quarter (Q1: April 15; Q2: July 15; Q3: October 15; Q4: January 15).
2. **Statistical Thresholds:** Testing shall utilize the Four-Fifths Rule (80% rule) and standard regression-based disparity analysis across all cohorts defined by protected attributes. If the selection rate for any protected cohort is less than 80% of the selection rate of the highest-scoring cohort, the model is classified as having a high disparate impact.
3. **Remediation Protocol:** Any model failing the quarterly disparate-impact test must be immediately transitioned to manual underwriting mode within 24 hours of report issuance. The model must be recalibrated, and disparate features removed, within 30 calendar days. Recalibrated models require sign-off from both the Chief Risk Officer and Chief Credit Officer before redeployment.

### 4.5 VOID DECISION RATIONALE RULE
Any credit decision—whether generated automatically by the algorithmic engine or manually by an underwriter—whose internal audit trail, scoring log, or underwriting memo references a protected attribute (Section 4.2) or a prohibited proxy (Section 4.3) as a material factor in the approval, pricing, or denial of credit is legally and operationally **void**.

1. **Mandatory Re-Run:** The original decision file must be purged from the active ledger within 2 business days of detection.
2. **Assignment:** The application file must be reassigned to a secondary, independent underwriting pod supervised by a Senior Credit Manager.
3. **Audit Log:** The system must record a compliance flag marked `VOID_PROXY_CONTAMINATION`, which is archived in the permanent compliance repository for regulatory inspection.

### 4.6 ADVERSE-ACTION NOTICE REQUIREMENTS
When a credit application is denied, counter-offered on less favorable terms, or closed for incompleteness, Ashva Capital must issue a formal Adverse-Action Notice to the applicant.

1. **Deadline:** The notice must be transmitted via secure electronic mail and physical postal mail no later than 5 business days following the final credit decision.
2. **Content Mandate:** The notice must explicitly state the principal, empirical reasons for the adverse action, up to a maximum of four distinct factors, ranked in descending order of significance.
3. **Prohibition on Proxies in Notices:** Under no circumstances shall an adverse-action notice cite, hint at, or utilize a protected attribute or prohibited proxy as a reason for denial. If the primary driver of a decline correlated with a prohibited feature, underwriters must select the closest orthogonal financial metric (e.g., Debt-to-Income ratio exceeding 45%, or credit bureau score falling below the Ashva Capital cut-off of 620).
4. **Contact Protocol:** The notice must include the direct contact details of the Ashva Capital Fair Lending Ombudsman (email: `fairlending@ashvacapital.internal`, telephone: 1-800-555-ASHVA) and inform the applicant of their right to request a copy of the credit scoring factors within 60 calendar days of notice receipt.
