# Sector Underwriting Note: Pharma Packaging

**ASHVA CAPITAL INTERNAL CREDIT POLICY MANUAL**
**SECTION 14: SECTOR-SPECIFIC UNDERWRITING STANDARDS — PHARMA PACKAGING SME**
**DOCUMENT REF: ACP-SEC-PP-042**
**EFFECTIVE DATE: 1. NOVEMBER 2023**

---

### 14.1 GENERAL APPLICABILITY AND SECTOR DEFINITION
14.1.1 This section governs all credit facilities extended to Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) operating in the Pharmaceutical Packaging sector, including manufacturers of blister packs, glass vials, aluminium foil seals, HDPE/LDPE bottles, and aseptic carton containers.
14.1.2 Underwriters must read this section in conjunction with the Ashva Capital General Underwriting Framework (AC-GUF-01). Where a conflict arises between general standards and this sector note, this note shall prevail.

### 14.2 WORKING CAPITAL CYCLE PARAMETERS
14.2.1 The typical working capital cycle for approved entities in this sector is **60 days**, calculated as Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) plus Days Inventory Outstanding (DIO) minus Days Payables Outstanding (DPO).
14.2.2 Underwriters must reject any application where the applicant's verified working capital cycle exceeds **85 days** on a trailing twelve-month (TTM) average basis, unless an explicit exception is granted by the Credit Committee under Clause 14.7.
14.2.3 Inventory valuation for working capital facility drawing base calculations must apply a standard haircut of **25%** on finished goods (due to client-specific tooling and artwork obsolescence) and **15%** on raw materials (polymer resins and pharmaceutical-grade aluminium foil).

### 14.3 DEBT SERVICE COVERAGE RATIO (DSCR) FLOOR
14.3.1 Notwithstanding the standard Ashva Capital DSCR floor of 1.25x set out in AC-GUF-01, the minimum acceptable Debt Service Coverage Ratio for all Pharma Packaging SME exposures shall be **1.30x**.
14.3.2 The 1.30x DSCR floor must be demonstrated on both historical TTM audited financials and projected cash flows for the entire tenor of the requested facility, incorporating a sensitivity stress test of a **15%** reduction in operating margins.

### 14.4 SEASONALITY ADJUSTMENTS
14.4.1 The Pharma Packaging sector exhibits **no marked season** across annual cycles, as production aligns with continuous pharmaceutical manufacturing schedules.
14.4.2 Consequently, underwriters shall not accept seasonal revenue peaks or troughs as justification for fluctuating drawing power limits or seasonal repayment holidays. Monthly debt service obligations must remain constant throughout the calendar year.

### 14.5 CAUSES OF DISTRESS AND MANDATORY MITIGANTS
14.5.1 The two most common causes of distress in this sector are:
1.  **Regulatory and Compliance Non-Conformance:** Failure of packaging materials to meet FDA, ISO 15378, or domestic drug regulatory authority standards, resulting in immediate product recalls by the pharmaceutical client base and catastrophic revenue cessation.
    *   *Mandatory Mitigant:* Underwriters must verify valid ISO 15378 certification and obtain copies of the last **two** client quality audits with zero critical observations.
2.  **Customer Concentration Risk:** Over-reliance on a single or dual generic drug manufacturer for more than **35%** of gross revenues, leaving the borrower vulnerable to contract loss or aggressive price renegotiation.
    *   *Mandatory Mitigant:* Any borrower with single-client concentration exceeding 35% must maintain minimum unencumbered liquid reserves equal to **3 months** of debt service at all times.

### 14.6 ACCEPTABLE COLLATERAL AND LENDING LIMITS
14.6.1 Acceptable collateral types are strictly limited to:
1.  First charge on plant and machinery (extrusion lines, blister forming machines, printing presses) valued by an Ashva Capital-empaneled valuer, subject to a maximum Loan-to-Value (LTV) of **65%**.
2.  First charge on commercial real estate (factory land and buildings), subject to a maximum LTV of **70%**.
3.  Hypothecation of book debts and inventory, subject to a maximum LTV of **50%** of drawing power.
14.6.2 The maximum aggregate exposure Ashva Capital will hold in the Pharma Packaging sector is **8%** of the total loan book. As of the effective date of this document, current exposure stands at **6.4%**, leaving an available sector headroom of **1.6%**.
14.6.3 The maximum allowable loan tenor for any facility under this sector note is **60 months**. Bullet repayment structures are prohibited; all facilities must amortize on a monthly basis.

### 14.7 NAMED EXCEPTIONS AND ESCALATION
14.7.1 Deviations from the DSCR floor of 1.30x, the maximum tenor of 60 months, or the sector exposure cap of 8% are strictly prohibited.
14.7.2 Exceptions to the working capital cycle ceiling (Clause 14.2.2) or maximum collateral LTVs may only be authorized jointly in writing by the Chief Credit Officer (Marcus Vance) and the Head of Portfolio Risk (Sarah Lin). All exception requests must be logged in the Ashva Capital Risk Register within **24 hours** of approval.
