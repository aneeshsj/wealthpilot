# Sector Underwriting Note: Food Processing

**ASHVA CAPITAL: INTERNAL CREDIT POLICY MANUAL**
**SECTION 14: SECTOR-SPECIFIC UNDERWRITING PROTOCOL – FOOD PROCESSING SMEs**
**DOCUMENT ID: CP-MFR-014-REV.4**

---

### 14.1 Scope and Application
14.1.1 This section governs the evaluation, structuring, and ongoing monitoring of all commercial credit facilities extended to Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) operating within the Food Processing sector. Underwriters must read this section in conjunction with Master Credit Policy CP-GEN-001.
14.1.2 Where sector-specific criteria detailed herein conflict with general underwriting guidelines, the provisions of this Section 14 shall take precedence.

### 14.2 Sector Portfolio Exposure Cap
14.2.1 The aggregate exposure of Ashva Capital to the Food Processing sector shall not exceed **12.0 percent** of the total active loan book value.
14.2.2 As of the last monthly portfolio rebalancing date (1st of the current month), the current sector exposure stands at **9.8 percent** of the total loan book, leaving an available allocation capacity of 2.2 percent.
14.2.3 Any loan application that would breach the 12.0 percent exposure cap must be automatically declined by the automated underwriting system unless accompanied by an explicit written override signed by Chief Credit Officer Marcus Vance.

### 14.3 Working Capital Cycle and Financial Benchmarks
14.3.1 The typical working capital cycle for approved food processing SMEs in this portfolio is defined as **45 days**, measured from raw material procurement and inventory holding through production, invoicing, and final cash collection.
14.3.2 Borrowers exhibiting a working capital cycle exceeding 75 days require mandatory working capital optimization covenants embedded in the facility agreement, requiring a reduction of the cycle to the 45-day benchmark within two semi-annual review periods.
14.3.3 The minimum Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) floor for all Food Processing SME facilities is **1.25x**, calculated on a post-tax, post-distribution basis over a trailing 12-month period. No underwriting exceptions or downward waivers to this 1.25x floor are permitted under any delegated authority tier.

### 14.4 Facility Structure and Tenor Limits
14.4.1 The maximum allowable facility tenor for term loans extended under this sector is **60 months**. Working capital revolving lines shall be structured on a 12-month renewable review cycle subject to annual collateral re-valuation.
14.4.2 Principal moratoriums exceeding 6 months from the date of first disbursement are prohibited.

### 14.5 Seasonality Adjustments and Peak Working Capital
14.5.1 The designated seasonal peak period for the Food Processing sector is defined as **October to January** inclusive, driven by agricultural harvest schedules and festive demand surges.
14.5.2 During the seasonal peak period (October to January), approved borrowers may draw upon a pre-approved seasonal working capital sub-limit not exceeding 25 percent of the primary revolving facility limit, without triggering a covenant breach for elevated utilization.
14.5.3 Repayment schedules for term facilities must account for cash flow troughs occurring during the off-season months of June through August. Underwriters may structure balloon or step-down repayment schedules provided that off-season debt service coverage is demonstrated via historical 3-year cash flow models.

### 14.6 Primary Causes of Distress
Underwriters must specifically assess and mitigate the two primary historical causes of distress identified in the Ashva Capital Food Processing portfolio:
14.6.1 **Raw Material Price Volatility (Margin Compression):** Unhedged exposure to agricultural commodity price fluctuations where processors cannot pass cost increases downstream to retail off-takers within a 30-day window. *Mitigation:* Underwriters must verify back-to-back supply contracts or enforce a minimum gross margin threshold of 18 percent.
14.6.2 **Perishable Inventory Spoilage and Cold Chain Failure:** Sudden loss of inventory value due to inadequate refrigeration, power grid unreliability, or logistic bottlenecks. *Mitigation:* Mandatory requirement for operational backup generators with automatic transfer switches and comprehensive stock-throughput insurance naming Ashva Capital as first loss payee.

### 14.7 Acceptable Collateral Types
Facilities must be secured by a combination of the following eligible collateral types, valued in accordance with Ashva Capital Valuation Standard VS-09:
14.7.1 **First-Ranking Hypothecation:** On all plant, machinery, processing equipment, and current assets (book debts and inventory), subject to a maximum advance rate of 65 percent for finished goods, 50 percent for raw materials, and 75 percent for verified book debts under 90 days.
14.7.2 **Immovable Property:** First legal mortgage over industrial land and factory buildings owned by the operating entity or promoter, maintaining a minimum collateral coverage ratio of 1.25x against the total facility limit.
14.7.3 **Personal Guarantees:** Joint and several personal guarantees from all promoters holding greater than 20 percent equity stake in the borrowing entity, backed by verified net worth statements audited by a chartered accountant.
